The first paddle (with the red insert) was printed from
https://github.com/sethpollen/drawings/commit/7c6d50800554b43e7207210d93c857a3612e8144.
It used the following settings:
  Top and Bottom pieces:
    11% infill
    4 floor layers + 2 layers of 50% lines
    1 ceiling layer
  Core piece:
    20% infill
    2 floor layers
    2 ceiling layers

In hindsight, I should have printed the core piece with more floor and ceiling
layers, and probably more fill. The paddle eventually broke because the core
piece split in half.

The second paddle (colorless) is 13mm thick and uses a finger joint. I printed
it using the same settings as the Top and Bottom pieces above.

I should probably to >20% infill for the grips, or they end up feeling kinda
soft. How about 25%.
